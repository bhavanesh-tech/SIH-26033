## Problem Title

SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers' earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creator's Organization

Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme

Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description

Farmers often receive low prices for their products because of middlemen. Consumers also have to pay higher prices.

Our project aims to solve this problem by connecting farmers directly with buyers through a simple website.

The website will:

- Connect farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
- Allow farmers to add products, prices, and quantities.
- Help buyers find products and contact farmers.
- Provide basic transportation support.

### Benefits

- Better prices for farmers.
- Lower prices for consumers.
- Reduced dependence on middlemen.

## Proposed Solution

### 1. Detailed explanation of the proposed solution

AgriLink is a simple website that connects farmers directly with buyers. Farmers can add their products, prices, and quantities. Buyers can view products and contact farmers directly.

### 2. How it addresses the problem

AgriLink helps farmers sell their products without depending on many middlemen. Buyers can purchase products directly from farmers, reducing extra costs.

### 3. Innovation and uniqueness of the solution

The main feature of AgriLink is direct communication between farmers and buyers. The website is simple and easy to use. Buyers can search for products and compare prices.

## Technical Approach

### 1. Technologies to be used

- HTML – To create the website structure.
- CSS – To design the website.
- JavaScript – To add product listing and searching features.

### 2. Methodology and process for implementation

1. Create a website for farmers and buyers.
2. Allow farmers to add products and prices.
3. Display available products on the website.
4. Allow buyers to search for products.
5. Provide farmer contact details for direct communication.

### Workflow

Farmer adds products → Products are displayed → Buyer searches for products → Buyer contacts farmer → Purchase.

## Feasibility and Viability

### 1. Analysis of the feasibility of the idea

The project is easy to develop using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It does not require expensive equipment. The website can be developed and tested using a computer and a web browser.

### 2. Potential challenges and risks

- Some farmers may not know how to use websites.
- Transportation may be difficult.
- Product prices may change frequently.

### 3. Strategies for overcoming these challenges

- Keep the website simple and user-friendly.
- Allow farmers to update their product details.
- Provide farmer contact details for direct communication.
- Help farmers coordinate transportation with buyers.

## Impact and Benefits

### 1. Potential impact on the target audience

AgriLink will help farmers, consumers, and bulk buyers connect easily. It will give farmers more opportunities to sell their products.

### 2. Benefits of the solution

**Social Benefits**

- Easy communication between farmers and buyers.
- Better opportunities for small farmers.

**Economic Benefits**

- Better prices for farmers.
- Lower prices for consumers.
- Reduced dependence on middlemen.

**Environmental Benefits**

- Better coordination of transportation.
- Reduced unnecessary transportation.
